Release History

Track new features, improvements, and fixes in each version

new_releases Version 0.8.2.2
September 1, 2026

build Bug Fixes

  • The panel opens on slow-loading emails - When Gmail hadn't finished rendering a message, the sidebar stayed dead on that email with no explanation. It now opens normally.
  • Attachments that don't make it are reported - If a file can't be attached to the record, a notice names it instead of failing silently.
  • One failed file no longer blocks the rest - When an attachment failed, the remaining files were never uploaded. Now they are.
new_releases Version 0.8.2.0
August 22, 2026

star New Features

  • Confirmed orders for the contactPLUS - A new section lists the sales orders already confirmed for the contact. Until now an order left the Quotations section the moment it was confirmed and appeared nowhere else, so emails about delivery, invoicing or issues could only be logged against the contact. Every row shows the order reference, its total and when it was placed, and you can log the email to the order.
new_releases Version 0.8.1.0
August 21, 2026

build Bug Fixes

  • Financial section on Odoo 19 - It loads again when you connect with an API key. Session-based connections were not affected.
  • Overdue amount - Calculated again on Odoo 19 online revisions (saas-19.1 and later). Odoo 19.0 was not affected.
  • Attachments - They upload with their content again on online revisions saas-19.3 and later. Earlier revisions were not affected.
  • Odoo error messages - They appear translated, and a rejected credential retries on its own.
  • Closed tasks - They no longer show up in the task list on Odoo 14–16.
  • Due amounts on Odoo 14 - Due and overdue amounts now appear on Odoo 14 Enterprise.
new_releases Version 0.8.0.0
August 1, 2026

star New Features

  • Actions menu on every record - Hover an opportunity, task or ticket to open a menu with the actions available for it: copy a direct link to the record, and switch auto-log on or off, which used to take up space in the row.
  • Quotations waiting on a decisionPLUS - A new section lists the contact's draft and sent quotations, split by whose move is next: not sent yet, or waiting on the customer. Every row shows the amount and how long it has been waiting or how soon it expires, and you can log the email to a quotation or start a new one and finish it in Odoo.

build Bug Fixes

  • Contacts are found whatever the capitalisation - An email stored in Odoo with capital letters now matches the sender and shows their record.

new_releases Version 0.5.0.0

star New Features

  • Auto-logging (private beta)PLUS - Stop logging every reply by hand:
    • Link a thread once and the next messages get logged automatically to your lead, task, ticket, or contact
    • One email can leave a trace on several records at once (a lead AND a ticket, for example)
    • See at a glance which emails you logged yourself and which came in automatically
    • Know at any time how many threads are being tracked
    • If something goes wrong, you'll see it in Settings before it becomes a problem
  • Multi-company supportPLUS - Work across all your Odoo companies without leaving Gmail:
    • Switch between your Odoo companies right from the sidebar header
    • Create leads, tasks, and tickets without picking the wrong company — they always go to the active one
    • Avoid duplicate contacts: if a customer already lives in another of your companies, the extension warns you before creating a new one
    • Decide what to do when a customer's company is in another Odoo: share, replicate it here, or create a standalone contact
